FAQ

Common questions

Who do you work with?

We work with small and mid-sized businesses, growing companies and startups across a range of industries, including healthcare, professional services, retail, manufacturing, technology, real estate and hospitality.

What happens in the free consultation?

It's a conversation of about 30 minutes about your business, your goals and the challenges you're facing. We'll share initial thoughts and, if it makes sense, suggest how we could help. There's no cost and no obligation.

Do you work in person or remotely?

Both. We're based in Texas and work with clients in person or remotely by video, whichever suits you best.

How is pricing structured?

It depends on the engagement. Projects are usually priced at a fixed fee for an agreed scope, and ongoing advisory is typically a monthly retainer. You'll always receive a clear written proposal with fees before any work begins.

How quickly can we get started?

Usually within a week or two of agreeing on a proposal, depending on the scope of the work.

Is our information kept confidential?

Yes. We treat all client information as confidential and are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before discussing details.
