Ways to Work Together

Engagements that fit your needs

Every engagement is tailored. These are the three most common ways clients work with us.

Project

A defined engagement with a clear goal and end date.

Best for a specific challenge such as a growth plan, a process redesign or a market study.

  • Fixed scope and timeline
  • Agreed deliverables
  • Handover and next-step plan

Ongoing Advisory

A regular advisory relationship, typically month to month.

Best for leaders who want an experienced sounding board and steady support while they grow.

  • Regular strategy sessions
  • On-call guidance between meetings
  • Progress tracking against goals

Workshop

A focused, facilitated session with your leadership team.

Best for aligning a team on priorities, strategy or a specific decision.

  • Pre-session preparation
  • Half-day or full-day session
  • Written summary and action plan
